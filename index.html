Agentifying the eReferral
Experience.

A Proof of Concept to accelerate time-to-care — turning unstructured clinical PDFs into booked service appointments, autonomously and in seconds.

The problem

~600 eReferrals a month. Every one processed by hand.

Hundreds of hours lost

Clinical data transcribed field by field — 600 referrals per month, entirely by hand.

Patients wait

Referrals received outside hours queue overnight and across weekends unprocessed.

Errors create compliance risk

Mandatory field errors generate SA Health breach notices — a direct regulatory consequence.

Clinical staff doing admin

Nurses and coordinators on data entry, not care — the highest-cost misallocation.

Clinical intelligence lost

Medications, comorbidities, clinical notes — buried in the PDF, invisible across the episode of care.

The impact: Delayed time-to-care for critical patients and severe compliance risk — including SA Health breach notices.

The smarter path

OCR + RPA automates the steps.
Agentforce understands the document.

The old way

OCR + RPA

Rigid, layout-dependent

Breaks the moment a referring hospital changes their template. Every new format requires a developer to remap the rules.

No comprehension — only characters

Can't tell "DOB", "D.O.B." and "born 14 March 1952" are the same thing. Clinical shorthand and free-text notes are invisible.

Fails silently on exceptions

A missing mandatory field crashes the workflow or writes bad data — with no ability to reason, escalate, or recover.

Follows rules. Can't handle the unknown.

Every edge case must be anticipated and coded. A referral format it's never seen before will always fail.

The new way

Agentforce + LLMs

Layout-agnostic by design

Reads a referral like a human does — understanding context, not matching pixels. Any template, any sender, zero re-engineering.

True clinical comprehension

Understands abbreviations, free-text notes, medications, comorbidities — surfacing the full clinical picture, not just the typed fields.

Reasons through exceptions

Missing a mandatory field? The agent detects it, decides what to do, and triggers the right action — a callback, a flag, a human review.

Handles cases it's never seen before

Reasoning replaces rules. A new referral format, a novel clinical scenario — the agent adapts without a developer rewriting anything.
