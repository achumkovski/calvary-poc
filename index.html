Agentifying the eReferral
Experience.

A Proof of Concept to accelerate time-to-care — turning unstructured clinical PDFs into booked service appointments, autonomously and in seconds.

The problem

~1,000 eReferrals a month.
Every one processed by hand.

Calvary Community Care processes ~1,000 SACC eReferrals per month — entirely by hand. A team of 3–4 FTEs reads, interprets, and transcribes clinical data from PDF documents into Salesforce, one field at a time.

The consequences are significant

Patients wait — referrals received outside business hours queue overnight and across weekends with no processing occurring

Errors create compliance risk — manual transcription of mandatory fields generates SA Health breach notices

Clinical staff are occupied with administration — nurses and coordinators spend their time on data entry, not care

Scale requires headcount — every increase in referral volume demands more people

The hidden cost

The referral documents themselves are a rich source of clinical intelligence — medication charts, comorbidities, functional assessments, clinical notes. In the manual process, that information is never extracted. It stays buried in the PDF, invisible to the care team, and lost to the patient record.

What this POC tests

Whether Agentforce + Salesforce Health Cloud can automate end-to-end SACC eReferral intake — from PDF ingestion through to appointment generation — while unlocking the clinical data the current process discards. Human-in-the-loop checkpoints are preserved where clinical safety demands it. Everything else runs 24 hours a day.

The question we set out to answer

How do we use Salesforce AI to transform
unstructured clinical PDFs into immediate action?

The answer: deploy three AI agents that handle 90% of the referral workflow autonomously — so Calvary's care team can focus entirely on the 10% that needs human empathy.

The agentic architecture

3 agents. 2 humans.

Top tier — Automated heavy lifting

Document Extract Agent

The 24/7 background worker. Ingests PDFs and maps complex clinical data into Health Cloud records instantly.

Call Back Agent

The outbound initiator. Uses telephony/SMS to chase providers for missing mandatory fields automatically.

Care Lead Assistive Agent

The in-workflow copilot. Summarises patient data on demand — so staff never chase a file again.

Bottom tier — Empathy & judgment

Care Lead

The last-mile scheduler. Intervenes for complex bookings requiring human empathy — patient calls, edge cases, and relationship nuance.

Care Manager

The strategic overseer. Monitors service performance, workloads, and SLA compliance across the Home Care portfolio.
