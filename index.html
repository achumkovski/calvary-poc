Agentifying the eReferral
Experience.

A Proof of Concept to accelerate time-to-care — turning unstructured clinical PDFs into booked service appointments, autonomously and in seconds.

The problem

~600 eReferrals a month. Every one processed by hand.

Hundreds of hours lost

Clinical data transcribed field by field — 600 referrals per month, entirely by hand.

Patients wait

Referrals received outside hours queue overnight and across weekends unprocessed.

Errors create compliance risk

Mandatory field errors generate SA Health breach notices — a direct regulatory consequence.

Clinical staff doing admin

Nurses and coordinators on data entry, not care — the highest-cost misallocation.

Clinical intelligence lost

Medications, comorbidities, clinical notes — buried in the PDF, invisible across the episode of care.

The impact: Delayed time-to-care for critical patients and severe compliance risk — including SA Health breach notices.

The question we set out to answer

How do we use Salesforce AI to transform
unstructured clinical PDFs into immediate action?

01

Faster time-to-care

02

Reduce admin burden

The agentic architecture

Live Agent

Document Extract Agent

The 24/7 background worker — ingests every inbound referral PDF without coordinator involvement.

▾
Ingests inbound referral PDFs from SA Health portal
Maps clinical fields directly to Health Cloud records
Routes referral — complete, pending, or incomplete
Operates 24/7 — no after-hours backlog
Live Agent

Care Lead Assistive Agent

The in-workflow copilot — surfaces patient context on demand so coordinators focus on decisions, not file hunting.

▾
Summarises patient data on demand for the Care Lead
Surfaces medications, comorbidities & clinical notes
Assists with complex bookings requiring human judgment
Reduces time spent searching across records
Future Agent

Call Back Agent

The outbound initiator — automatically contacts providers when mandatory fields are missing from a referral.

▾
Detects missing mandatory fields — DOB, Medicare ID, IRN
Initiates outbound call or SMS to the referring provider
Updates the referral record once information is received
Eliminates manual provider chasing by coordinators

Humans — empathy & judgment

Care Lead

Last-mile scheduler. Intervenes for complex bookings requiring patient empathy and clinical judgment.

Care Manager

Strategic overseer. Monitors service performance, workloads, and SLA compliance across the portfolio.

01

Scalability

This architecture is ring-fenced for SACC today — but every component is provider-agnostic. Calvary operates across hospitals, aged care, and community health. The same flow deploys to every referral stream.

One architecture. Any provider, any referral stream.

02

Re-usability

The agents built here aren't single-purpose. Clone and redeploy them anywhere documents need reading, or any workflow needs an outbound voice.

Build once, deploy everywhere healthcare has paper or a phone.

03

Flexibility

Salesforce Headless 360 means the agentic experience isn't locked to Salesforce UI. Deliver it inside Teams, on an iPad, in any surface — without rebuilding the logic.

Agentforce inside Microsoft Teams — any surface, same intelligence.
