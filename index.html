Why this — why now

Friction in the referral funnel
costs patients time they don't have.

Manual PDF retrieval

3–4 FTEs process ~1,000 SA Health referrals per month — pulling PDFs from portals by hand, one by one.

Error-prone transcription

Clinical data is typed in manually — laborious and error-prone, with no automated validation against mandatory fields.

Missing mandatory fields

DOB, Medicare ID, and IRN are frequently absent. Staff chase providers manually — delaying triage and creating compliance risk.

After-hours backlogs

Referrals received outside 7am–8pm go unprocessed until morning — urgent patients wait overnight for acknowledgement.

The impact: Delayed time-to-care for critical patients and severe compliance risk — including SA Health breach notices.

The agentic architecture

3 agents. 2 humans.

Top tier — Automated heavy lifting

Document Extract Agent

The 24/7 background worker. Ingests PDFs and maps complex clinical data into Health Cloud records instantly.

Call Back Agent

The outbound initiator. Uses telephony/SMS to chase providers for missing mandatory fields automatically.

Care Lead Assistive Agent

The in-workflow copilot. Summarises patient data on demand — so staff never chase a file again.

Bottom tier — Empathy & judgment

Care Lead

The last-mile scheduler. Intervenes for complex bookings requiring human empathy — patient calls, edge cases, and relationship nuance.

Care Manager

The strategic overseer. Monitors service performance, workloads, and SLA compliance across the Home Care portfolio.

Concept to action — the referral flow

Three paths. One outcome.

!

Incomplete — missing mandatory fields

Document Extract Agent detects gaps (DOB, Medicare ID, IRN). Call Back Agent fires automatically — outbound telephony/SMS to provider to resubmit. Zero manual chasing.

~

Processed & pending — complex booking

Records generated, data extracted. Care Lead queries the Assistive Agent, calls the patient, and completes the booking with full context at their fingertips.

✓

Processed & booked — the happy path

Perfect extraction. All records created. Service appointment booked autonomously. Zero human intervention. Time from receipt to booking: seconds.

The Document Extract Agent sits at the centre — ingesting every inbound PDF and routing it to the right path without human triage.
