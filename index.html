Agentifying the eReferral
Experience.

A Proof of Concept to accelerate time-to-care — turning unstructured clinical PDFs into booked service appointments, autonomously and in seconds.

The problem

~600 eReferrals a month.
Every one processed by hand.

Hundreds of hours lost to manual processing

Clinical data transcribed field by field from PDFs into Salesforce — 600 referrals per month, entirely by hand.

Patients wait

Referrals received outside business hours queue overnight and across weekends with no processing occurring.

Errors create compliance risk

Manual transcription of mandatory fields generates SA Health breach notices — a direct regulatory consequence.

Clinical staff doing admin

Nurses and coordinators spend their time on data entry, not care — the highest-cost misallocation in the workflow.

Clinical intelligence buried and lost

Medication charts, comorbidities, and clinical notes stay buried in the PDF — invisible to the care team across the entire episode of care.

The impact: Delayed time-to-care for critical patients and severe compliance risk — including SA Health breach notices.
